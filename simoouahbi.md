## 👋 Hello

Hello! I'm **Mohamed Ouahbi**, a passionate self-taught **Full-Stack Developer** with a strong interest in software engineering, web architecture, frontend experiences, and continuous improvement.

My journey in development is driven by curiosity, discipline, and the desire to understand how things work — not simply how to use them.

I believe that becoming a better developer means going beyond writing code. It means understanding the architecture behind every component, respecting the technologies and conventions of an existing project, identifying weaknesses, solving real problems, and continuously refining the result.

I enjoy exploring unfamiliar codebases, learning new technologies, improving existing systems, and transforming ideas into functional experiences.

## 🌟 About My Work

I started the ECO-Inspired AcademicPages Evolution by exploring the original repository, understanding its architecture, examining its files and directories, and identifying how content, layouts, components, data, and styling work together.

My objective was not to replace the original project with another framework. It was to understand its existing engineering foundations and progressively evolve its design and functionality while respecting its architecture.
