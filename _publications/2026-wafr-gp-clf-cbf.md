We propose a composable, model-free reinforcement learning approach for navigation with unknown, nonlinear, input-affine systems, learning reusable reach and avoid behaviors for individual goals and obstacles (using Gaussian-process interpolation) and composing them through a QCQP. This yields formal obstacle-avoidance guarantees and provides a model-free alternative to CLF/CBF-based controllers.
